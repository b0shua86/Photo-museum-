Camera Obscura — audio (local MP3, fully offline)
=================================================

The museum never downloads or streams anything. Era music and the Kyle Thompson
wing playlist are read from local MP3 files placed in THIS folder. If a file is
absent the museum simply plays silence there — nothing breaks.

Drop your own MP3s here (you supply them — nothing copyrighted ships with the app):

  audio/movements/<movementId>.mp3   ← looping era ambience, crossfaded by hall
      movements/origins.mp3          (Invention & Origins, 1826–1860)
      movements/pictorialism.mp3     (Pictorialism)
      movements/straight.mp3         (Straight Photography & Modernism)
      movements/documentary.mp3      (Documentary & Social Realism)
      movements/street.mp3           (Street Photography)
      movements/color.mp3            (The New Color)
      movements/topographics.mp3     (New Topographics)
      movements/staged.mp3           (Staged & Contemporary Surrealism)

  audio/kyle-thompson/1.mp3 … 5.mp3  ← the flagship wing's 5-track playlist
                                       (plays in order while you're in Kyle's wing)

Notes
- The web prototype listed era tracks (Chopin, Satie, '20s jazz, '50s cool jazz,
  bossa nova, Eno, downtempo) and Dead Meadow for Kyle's wing as YouTube links.
  Those are NOT bundled — for an offline app, drop equivalent files you own here.
- Any standard MP3 works. Filenames must match exactly (lowercase).
- Toggle music in-app with the "Music" button or the P key.
